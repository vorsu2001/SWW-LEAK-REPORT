Seen water coming out of a road, pavement or field? Show us where it is and we'll get it fixed. It takes about 2 minutes.

Already reported? You'll see nearby leaks on the map and can join that case for text or email updates.

Is it an emergency?

If water is flooding a home or road, or it's causing danger, call us on [EMERGENCY NUMBER] instead of using this form.

Is it on your property?

Leaks inside your home or on your own supply pipe are usually yours to fix. This form is for leaks in roads, pavements, verges and fields.

What you'll need

  • Where the leak is (you'll drop a pin on a map)
  • What it looks like and how fast it's flowing
  • A photo, if you can take one safely
